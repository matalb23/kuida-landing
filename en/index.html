Kuida

Kuida supports therapy, medication and moments of calm for children with chronic pain — and for adults with neuropathy too. One day at a time, everything is logged and shared with the whole family.

⬇️ Download for Android (APK)

Free · Direct install (APK) · Requires Android 8 or later

🦋 What makes Kuida special

Kuida is here to accompany — never to replace — your healthcare team's plan. Every module is designed to make daily routines gentler, for both you and your child.

🦶

Little Feet

Short physical and sensory therapy routines, step by step, with explanations of what we do and why — for kids and for grown-ups.

🪜

Texture Ladder

Graded desensitization across six rungs, from silk to a caring hand. Everyone moves at their own pace, with no rush and no pressure.

🧠

Brain Training

Graded motor imagery: recognizing, imagining, mirroring and gentle movement. Pain science in the service of play.

💊

Clear medication

Reminders for every dose, a complete history, and contextual help to understand what each medication is for.

📊

Pain logging

Check-ins with faces, body maps and sensations. An honest journal to bring to every appointment.

🩺

Doctor's visits

A notebook for medical appointments: date, professional, what mattered in each visit and your questions for the next one. With a PDF summary to bring to the appointment.

📔

Family journal

Everything gets logged and shared among caregivers: who gave the medication, which therapy was done, how the day went.

🧘

The Refuge

Stories to read aloud, riddles, word-game cards, breathing with Lila, the 5-4-3-2-1 exercise and empowerment messages for hard days.

🔍

Neural Network Detective

Missions that explain how pain works, calm the body's false alarms, and ease the way back to school and to sleep.

🥚

Lila, the companion

An egg that grows with every step: caterpillar, chrysalis and butterfly. It celebrates effort, never results.

👨‍👩‍👧

A connected family

Everything syncs across the family's phones. Whoever is caring, always knows.

💛 Why Kuida exists

Kuida wasn't born as an app for the world: it was born as a tool for one family. Mine.

When my daughter's foot started to burn and itch with no visible cause, we learned that pediatric chronic pain is real, that it is hard to explain, and even harder to live alongside. That "it doesn't hurt today" is good news — and that "today my body said enough" is valuable information too.

Between physiotherapy, medication and play-based therapy, daily life became an enormous challenge. Kuida brings together everything we learned along the way: short, meaningful routines; an honest pain journal; a refuge of calm for the hard days; and a companion that celebrates every step.

I built it for my family, and I'm sharing it because I know there are more families like ours. If Kuida helps even one of them, every line of code was worth it.

— The family behind Kuida

📥 How to install

Kuida is distributed as an APK, so Android asks for one extra permission the first time. It's safe and takes less than a minute.

Download the APK

Tap the download button and pick the latest release on GitHub.

Allow unknown apps

When you open the file, Android will warn you. Open the settings and enable "Allow from this source" for your browser.

Install and open Kuida

Tap "Install" and you're done. On first launch you can create your child's profile and invite the family.
